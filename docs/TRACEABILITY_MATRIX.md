### M6 calibration compatibility rule / M6 校准兼容规则

- `BenchmarkMetrics.resourceUsage` and `ValidationInfo.resourceUsage` are optional `ResourceUsageEvidence` v1. Legacy `memoryPeakBytes` remains deprecated absolute VRAM semantics and is never compared with `estimate.totalMemoryBytes`.
